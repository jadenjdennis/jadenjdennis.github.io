# Surface brief: homepage and subpages

Mode: Experience (portfolio and personal space; the person leads). Brief-pinned world (CLAUDE.md): no direction roll was run.

## Audience and job
Visitors who want to meet Jaden Dennis: who he is, what he is exploring, where he is heading. Skim in minutes, then follow GitHub, LinkedIn or email. Content is honest and unfinished; unknowns are marked TODO, never invented.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal site built like the reading copy of a notebook: one big word ("Jaden"), a portrait set off-axis, and an index in place of a menu. It refuses the portfolio template (centred hero, circular headshot, card grid, skill bars).

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground, warm-white ink, sage for marks and links, jade as the single filled field (the contact close). Young Serif headings, Hanken Grotesk text. Hairline rules, a left margin column with sticky section titles, a dotted-leader index, square timeline markers. No cards, gradients, glass or shadows.

STORY: In one scroll the visitor learns who Jaden is, what he is exploring and where he is heading, believes it because the gaps are marked, and reaches out.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Thin top bar: "Jaden Dennis" small at left; theme toggle and the Index disclosure at right. Hero: "Jaden" at roughly 9-10rem across eight columns; the curiosity intro paragraph indented below at about 36ch; a 4:5 portrait frame at the right, dropped below the name's top edge, with crop marks and a quiet caption. The Index control is the primary action; a scroll cue sits bottom-left.

FORM: Book index and editorial notebook, pinned by the brief; position n/a; seed: none (unavailable).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment
The Index: a book-style table of contents with dotted leaders that unfolds from the top bar and marks where you are.
